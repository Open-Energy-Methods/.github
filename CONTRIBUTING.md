# Contributing to Open Energy Methods

We welcome independent verification cases, error reports, tests, examples, documentation, and scientific critique.

## Before contributing

- Search existing issues and check the relevant repository's scope and license.
- Explain the scientific purpose of a proposed numerical check.
- Include an independently derived expected result or an explicit reason one is unavailable.
- Identify assumptions, units, reference frame, sign convention, tolerances, and limitations.
- Distinguish synthetic, simulated, measured, and externally published evidence.
- Do not contribute unpublished, confidential, restricted, or unlicensed research material.

## Pull requests

Keep each change reviewable. For scientific changes provide a valid reference case, at least one appropriate negative control, and tests. Do not weaken checks to make a benchmark pass without documenting why.

Discussion and acceptance of a contribution do not imply KTH or other institutional endorsement. See the individual project for its license and additional requirements.
