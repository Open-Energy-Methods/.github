# Community Code of Conduct

We expect professional, respectful, evidence-based discussion across academic, industrial, and volunteer contributors.

Disagreement about methods and results is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and disclosure of private research information are not.

Report concerning behavior privately to the organization maintainers through GitHub's private reporting channels where available. If no private channel is configured, do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers may moderate discussions and contributions to protect community participation and research integrity.

This brief policy will be reviewed alongside a formal community governance policy as the contributor community develops.
