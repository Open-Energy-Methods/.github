This repository keeps the housekeeping for **Open Energy Methods**: its public profile, contribution guidelines, brand files and project organization. The interesting work is the engineering, not the administrative scaffolding.

**[Visit the workbench and read the field notes](https://open-energy-methods.github.io/)** · **[Read the Creator & Maintainer profile](profile/README.md)**

If you're writing about the project, start with [the editorial voice and evidence guide](docs/EDITORIAL_VOICE.md): show a specific question, a reproducible result, and what it cannot establish.

The GitHub profile is maintained in [`profile/README.md`](profile/README.md). The workbench is created and maintained by Abhijith Sivaprasadan. Its approved navy/teal visual identity and vector assets are kept in [`assets/brand/`](assets/brand/).

OEM is an independent open-source engineering research workbench, not an incorporated entity, a university unit, or a certification authority.

## Current working direction

For at least the next year, prioritize published projects, reproducible experiments, transparent methods, technical field notes and approachable open-source maintenance. Formation of a company, selling services, recruiting a team or demonstrating commercial demand is **not a required outcome**. That question can be revisited if the work and circumstances justify it.
